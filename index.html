    Where · When · Who

    Across Sudan. Ongoing since the war began. Women, men and youth, women most of all.

    Why it occurs

    Unequal access to education, jobs, finance and land; regional neglect; conflict.

    Why it matters

    It deepens poverty and weakens social stability. Solutions point to education, women’s property rights, finance and public services.

    07 Empathy map

    A week in her shoes

    We empathise with a displaced woman from Darfur or Kordofan. She is the breadwinner, the carer and often a volunteer, caught between the war and a system that already gave her fewer rights than men.

    Story

    Make her choices

    Day 1 of 5

    Her week

    There was no good option, only trade-offs. That is the gap the Equal Opportunity Program is built to close: learning support so school is not traded for food, skills so income does not depend on prices she cannot control.

    Stamp collected.

    She sees

    The pound past 8,000 to the dollar. Schools used as shelters. Community kitchens closing after USAID was dismantled.

    She hears

    Which areas are safe, which kitchens are open, and that funding for women-led groups is being cut.

    She does

    Survives and organises. Runs kitchens and safe spaces. Homeschools, or pulls children from school.

    • Peace and a safe return home
    • Equal rights, as the 2019 Constitutional Declaration promises

    10 Our solution

    The Equal Opportunity Program

    A program that helps disadvantaged women and youth build skills and reach the resources they need. We plan a six-month pilot: a skills hub in Kigali for Sudanese refugees and a partner-led cohort inside Sudan.

    Video · Our solutionThe Equal Opportunity ProgramHow our program helps women and youth build skills and reach opportunity.Watch on YouTube ↗
    Skills track

    10 weeks of digital literacy, bookkeeping and a market trade.

    Learning support

    Stipends for transport, data and childcare so women can stay enrolled.

    Pathway to income

    Mentors, employer links, and starter kits for the best business plans.

    Community circles

    Open sessions with families and leaders on gender roles and women’s work.
